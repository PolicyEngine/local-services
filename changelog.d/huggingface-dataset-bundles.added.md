Add `stage_dataset_bundle`, `dataset_bundle_receipt` and `check_write_access`. A build hands over its staged dataset bundle with each file's role; the emitter writes it under `staged/<run_id>/` in one commit with Microcosm's message, paths and idempotency (`already_staged`, `REMOTE_DIFFERS`), and retries Hub failures from the durable queue. The emitter copies the bundle's small files (each up to 32 MiB, up to 64 MiB per hand-over) into its queue before answering, so the build may rewrite them at once, as Microcosm does with its build manifest, checksum listing and build record; it uploads larger files from their paths and never uploads one whose bytes changed after the hand-over.
