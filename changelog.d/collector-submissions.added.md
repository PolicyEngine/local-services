Submit staged bundles' calibration diagnostics and Orrery inputs to the collector's artifact intake, off by default: a bundle hand-over may carry `CollectorProvenance` (country, build, licensed data, release), and with `collector_submissions` switched on the `huggingface` module submits the files it names after the bundle's Hugging Face commit, through the `artifacts:submit` credential, signed uploads and completion, retrying outages from the durable hand-over queue. `collector_submission_receipt` reports the result. It follows collector#6's contract at `673df3d`: a token is used only when the exchange grants `artifacts:submit`, a collector without intake leaves the hand-over pending for an hour, and a rejected completion fails it. Calibration data may be public; only the Orrery outputs of licensed builds stay private until cleared, and native evidence is never published.
