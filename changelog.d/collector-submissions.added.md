Submit staged bundles' calibration diagnostics and Orrery inputs to the collector's artifact intake, off by default: a bundle hand-over may carry `CollectorProvenance` (country, build, licensed data, release), and with `collector_submissions` switched on the `huggingface` module submits the files it names after the bundle's Hugging Face commit, through the `artifacts:submit` credential, signed uploads and completion, retrying outages from the durable hand-over queue. `collector_submission_receipt` reports the result.
