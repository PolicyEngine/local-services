Pull requests now check the repository's rules registry. `rules/registry.toml` lists the repository's registered rules; the "Rules registry" job runs the rules tool's tests and `python -m scripts.rules guard`, which fails when a rule names a check that does not exist, or that runs only in jobs the required check does not wait for, that may be skipped on pull requests or whose failure does not fail the run, when CI runs a check no rule describes, or when a rule or its list of tolerated violations is weaker than on `main`. `python -m scripts.rules render` writes `docs/generated/rules.md` and the Rules section of `AGENTS.md`. The tool is a copy of the simulation API's, reading what differs between repositories from the registry's `[repository]` table. The wheels are unchanged, the build leaves the tool, the registry and their documents out of the `microcosm-emitter` source archive, and the artifact check refuses an archive that holds them.
