Pull requests now check the repository's rules registry. `rules/registry.toml` lists the rules that govern this repository; the "Rules registry" job runs the rules tool's tests and `python -m scripts.rules guard`, which fails when a rule names a check that does not exist or that no job the required check waits for runs, when CI runs a check no rule describes, or when a rule or its list of tolerated violations is weaker than on `main`. `python -m scripts.rules render` writes `docs/generated/rules.md` and the Rules section of `AGENTS.md`. The tool is a copy of the simulation API's, reading what differs between repositories from the registry's `[repository]` table. The wheels are unchanged; the `microcosm-emitter` source archive, which already carries the tests and tools, now also carries the tool, the registry and its documents.
